Installation complete / 安装完成

Open EndfieldBridge from the Start Menu when ready. Read INSTALL.md and the risk
documents. Select your own game and loader paths; Mod mode requires explicit setup.
Before uninstalling or moving, use Restore EFMI while the bridge is still present.
Uninstall preserves user-created settings/backups and does not restore XXMI itself.
